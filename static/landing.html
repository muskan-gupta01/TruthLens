OUR SOLUTIONS

Secure digital verification

TruthLens provides intelligent tools to verify users, check documents, and help protect platforms against fraud.

🪪

Identity Verification

Verify users securely through identity information checks, mathematical checksums, and MRZ standard compliance.

📄

Document Screening

Analyze travel documents optically and forensically to detect altered text, invalid fields, and tampered photos.

🛡️

Fraud Detection

Use Error Level Analysis (ELA), cross-validation, and 128-D biometric face matching to identify suspicious activity.

HOW IT WORKS

Verification in three simple steps

TruthLens makes identity and document verification rapid, accurate, and seamless.

1

Enter Details

The officer or passenger uploads an identification document and an optional live photo.

2

AI Analysis

Our multi-layer forensic engine runs OCR, checksum validation, ELA tampering checks, and 1:1 face matching.

3

Get Result

Receive a comprehensive explainable risk audit, pass/review verdict, and downloadable border certificate.
