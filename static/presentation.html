Border Checkpoint Identity Screening Strain

Operational Bottlenecks

  • High Passenger Volume: Border checkpoints process thousands of passports, visas, and national IDs daily.

  • Counterfeit Travel Documents: Fabricated documents lacking mathematical check digit compliance.
  • Blacklisted Travel Documents: Use of stolen blank passport stock (Interpol notices).
